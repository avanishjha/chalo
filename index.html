Culture · Trip 01

The Golden Fort

Jaisalmer and the Thar

Jaisalmer, Rajasthan. People still live inside the fort.

Welcome to my city.

I’m Ek. One hump. That’s what Ek means, in Hindi.

Sam dunes, about 40 km out.

Tourists bring snacks. I like tourists.

THUD.

After dark: folk music and a fire.

I don’t dance. I’ve been asked.

DHA DHIN!

Tents with proper beds. Yes, really.

I sleep outside. Obviously.

They say there’s another desert. Up north. With snow.

Blue City, Lake City

Jodhpur and Udaipur

Mehrangarh, high on its rock above the blue city.

Nobody in Jodhpur has seen this snow desert either.

Horses. Always showing off.

Gate after gate on the way up. Wear good shoes.

I don’t own shoes.

CREAK!

Every window here comes with a view.

She says keep going north.

Udaipur. Palaces on the lake.

Wrong way. Nice, though.

He rode to Udaipur. You get a car.

The Pink City

Jaipur and Amber

City tours start here. Amber, above Maota Lake.

Still no snow.

We don’t do elephant rides.

He drove his own elephant. I walked.

Festivals too. Come in March for Holi. Wear old clothes.

I was beige when I got here.

CHHAPAK!

Heritage walks. Old lanes, older doors.

Too narrow for me. You go.

Royal transfers, then.

Today: a car, a driver and a good chai stop.

Nobody offered me a car.

You walk. I’ll take the road.

POM POM!Horn OK please

Morning at high camp.

Why is the ground white?

The Other Desert

Leh, Nubra and Pangong

Leh, about 3,500 m. At least 48 hours of rest first. It’s the rule.

Best rule yet.

Hunder dunes, Nubra Valley.

Two humps?

I’m Do. It means two. It’s cold, so I pack lunch.

Pangong, about 4,250 m. Bring a jacket.

Do says this is warm.

BRRR.

Hanle: India’s first dark sky reserve.

OK. This one’s worth the walk.

Rest stops are part of the plan.

Finally, someone gets it.

Questions people ask first

Have you done this before?
Since 2009. We have mostly been showing India’s culture and heritage to travellers from abroad, with local partners we trust. The mountains are the new part, and we will say so plainly.
What does it cost?
For now, a surprise. We are still drawing up the price list. Send us your dates and group size, and you get a real number on WhatsApp.
Can you arrange all of it?
Yes. Customised tours, hotels, transport, the whole trip. Or just the parts you want.
Is Ladakh safe for my parents?
Altitude is the real question. Leh requires at least 48 hours of rest before going higher, and we build that in. Anyone with heart or lung conditions should check with their doctor first.
Do I need a permit?
Visitors from abroad need a Protected Area Permit for places like Nubra and Pangong; we arrange it. Indian visitors pay an online environment fee. [Client to confirm the current rules.]

Every trek and climb has a grade and a fitness check. [Grade scale and fitness test to confirm.]
