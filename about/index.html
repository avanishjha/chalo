About us

Since 2009

We are Indian Dream Travel. For years we have shown India’s culture, heritage and local life to travellers from abroad. Now we are taking the same care up the Himalaya.

See how we got here
  1. 2009

    We start out in cultural tourism, showing India to travellers from abroad.

  2. Then

    Heritage, local life, the places a guidebook skips. That is still the job.

  3. Along the way

    A network of local partners we trust, built one trip at a time.

  4. All of it

    Customised tours, places to stay, transport. The complete trip, not just the fort.

  5. Now

    The next step: the Indian Himalaya. Treks, expeditions and summit climbs.

Two sides, one team

The culture side is where we come from. The adventure side is where we are heading.
